Meet NOVA STUDIO

Built around real people and lasting progress.

Since 2018, NOVA STUDIO has helped people build stronger bodies, steadier habits, and more confidence in everyday movement.

Our story

Eight years of showing up together.

NOVA STUDIO began with a simple idea: fitness should feel welcoming, personal, and useful beyond the gym. Our coaches combine thoughtful programming with practical guidance, so every session has a clear purpose and every member can work at the right level.

We celebrate progress that lasts. That means meeting you where you are, adapting when life changes, and building routines that support your goals rather than taking over your life.
